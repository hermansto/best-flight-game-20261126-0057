A little red-and-white prop plane, a chain of green islands, and a sky full of
rings. **Merpati — Archipelago Run** is a complete flight game that runs
offline in desktop Chrome from static files: chase view over the sea, lit
instruments (altitude, speed, heading, vertical speed, fuel, compass and
attitude ball), a home runway, and three missions — Ring Run (thread all 8
rings, then land), Island Hop (take off, circle, bring her home) and Free
Flight. Throttle, pitch, roll and rudder on keyboard or mouse, with fuel and
damage turning every sortie into decisions, and synthesised engine, wind and
ring-chime audio from WebAudio. No network requests, no libraries — just
canvas, and the sky.
